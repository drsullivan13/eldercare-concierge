164 included Bergen agency offices were joined to their state registry legal name, registered DBA, ZIP and street. The roster produced 222 normalized office/name combinations. The county-only replay screened those aliases against 62,069 unique MyLivingChoice public property slugs (national sitemap, cached) using exact normalized names and local qualifiers. All 40 distinct slug candidates had already been inspected. A match to a name remains a lead until branch address, permitted use and review population are confirmed. This is a catalog search, not 164 independent internet searches. It does not establish that the other offices lack reviews. Geographic source alternate names and pages outside the sitemap remain possible.
