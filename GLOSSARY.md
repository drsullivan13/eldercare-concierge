# Harbor terms

| Term              | Meaning                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Provider          | One catalog record for a facility, campus, agency or staffing organization, identified by a stable provider ID.                     |
| Care unit         | A separately licensed or certified setting. Its evidence must not be assigned to an entire campus or brand.                         |
| CCN               | CMS certification number for a specific reporting facility or agency. It is distinct from a Harbor provider ID.                     |
| Catalog           | The reviewed discovery records used by ZIP/radius search. Inclusion is not an endorsement or a claim of availability.               |
| Research snapshot | Source-linked facts and review observations approved for display. Rebuilding it does not fetch new evidence.                        |
| Experience score  | The provisional zero-to-ten summary of eligible published consumer review averages. It does not measure clinical quality or safety. |
| Source family     | Sources known to share or republish review material. One family contributes once to a score.                                        |
| Facebook finding  | A selected, attributed qualitative account or recommendation. It never changes the numeric experience score.                        |
| Workspace         | The family's saved provider IDs, notes, profile and planning tasks. The current app stores it in the browser.                       |
| Shortlist         | The provider IDs saved in the workspace, even while their current catalog details are still loading.                                |
| Coverage          | What research has been attempted or retained. A gap does not imply poor care or an absence of reviews.                              |
| Review candidate  | A private collection result awaiting assessment. Collection and publication are separate steps.                                     |
